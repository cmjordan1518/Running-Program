Recommended Week

  • Monday — Easy Run

    3–4 mi conversational pace. Form, not speed.

  • Tuesday — Tempo

    5–6 mi with 2–3 mi at comfortably hard pace.

  • Wednesday — Rest/Cross

    Cycling, swimming, or strength. Non-negotiable.

  • Thursday — Base Run

    4–5 mi easy + 4×20-sec strides at the end.

  • Friday — Moderate

    4–5 mi at medium effort. Your feel-good run.

  • Saturday — Long Run

    8–12 mi easy. Highest-leverage session for 750.

  • Sunday — Full Rest

    Mandatory. Adaptation happens in recovery.

Season Phases

Jan–Mar: Base ✓

181 mi logged. Pace improved 8'56" → 8'34". Done.

Apr–Jun: Build ← NOW

Long run → 10–12 mi. Weekly target 17–20 mi.

Jul–Sep: Peak

Alabama heat: run before 7am or after 7pm. Effort over pace.

Oct–Dec: Finish

Bank the miles. Consider a fall half marathon.

📐 10% Rule: Never increase weekly mileage more than 10% week over week. Your body adapts in 2–3 week cycles.
⚠️ At 750 miles, injury prevention is your training plan. Sharp pain = stop immediately.
Prevention

🦵 IT Band

Clamshells, band walks, single-leg squats 3×/week.

🦶 Plantar

Daily calf raises. Frozen bottle roll after long runs.

🏃 Shin Splints

Vary surfaces. Replace shoes at 300–400 miles.

💧 Fueling

60+ min runs: 30–60g carbs/hr. Post-run protein within 30 min.

😴 Sleep

7–9 hrs is the #1 recovery tool. Non-negotiable.

🌡️ Alabama Heat

Before 7am or after 7pm. Electrolytes every run over 4 mi.

Daily Protocols

  • Pre-Run Warm-Up (5 min)

    Leg swings, hip circles, high knees, lunges. Never static stretch cold.

  • Post-Run Stretch (10 min)

    Calves, hip flexors, quads, hamstrings. 30–45 sec holds.

  • Foam Roll (3–4×/week)

    IT band, calves, quads, glutes. Pause on tender spots.

  • Strength (2×/week)

    Single-leg deadlifts, step-ups, hip bridges, calf raises.

👟 Shoe Tracking: At ~15 mi/week, you'll need new shoes around August. Replace at 300–400 miles.
