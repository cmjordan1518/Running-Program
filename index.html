Recommended Week

  • Monday — Easy Run

    3–4 mi conversational pace. Form, not speed.

  • Tuesday — Tempo

    5–6 mi with 2–3 mi comfortably hard.

  • Wednesday — Rest/Cross

    Cycling, swimming, or strength.

  • Thursday — Base Run

    4–5 mi easy + 4×20-sec strides.

  • Friday — Moderate

    4–5 mi medium effort. Feel-good run.

  • Saturday — Long Run

    8–12 mi easy. Highest-leverage session.

  • Sunday — Full Rest

    Mandatory. Adaptation happens here.

181 mi. Pace improved 8'56" → 8'34".

Apr–Jun: Build ← NOW

📐10% Rule: Never increase weekly mileage more than 10% week over week.
⚠️At 750 miles, injury prevention is your training plan. Sharp pain = stop immediately.
Prevention

🦵 IT Band

Clamshells, band walks, single-leg squats 3×/week.

🦶 Plantar

Daily calf raises. Frozen bottle roll post long run.

🏃 Shins

Vary surfaces. Replace shoes at 300–400 miles.

💧 Fueling

60+ min: 30–60g carbs/hr. Post-run protein within 30 min.

😴 Sleep

7–9 hrs is the #1 recovery tool.

🌡️ Heat

Before 7am or after 7pm. Electrolytes every run 4+ mi.

Daily Protocols

  • Pre-Run (5 min)

    Leg swings, hip circles, high knees, lunges. No cold static stretching.

  • Post-Run (10 min)

    Calves, hip flexors, quads, hamstrings. 30–45 sec holds.

  • Foam Roll (3–4×/wk)

    IT band, calves, quads, glutes. Pause on tender spots.

  • Strength (2×/wk)

    Single-leg deadlifts, step-ups, hip bridges, calf raises.

👟Shoe Tracking: At ~15 mi/week you will need new shoes around August. Replace at 300–400 miles.
